Seconds

Privacy Policy

Seconds does not collect any personal data. Your photos are analyzed on your iPhone and never leave it. Last updated 9 November 2026.

Who we are

Seconds is developed by Newconic Ventures (Sven Ahrens, Berlin, Germany), the controller for any personal data described here. You can reach us through the contact form.

Your photos

Seconds asks for access to your photo library so it can find duplicates and bursts. It analyzes small previews of your photos on your device, using Apple's on-device frameworks. No photo, preview or analysis result is uploaded to us or to anyone else.

If you use iCloud Photos and a preview is not stored on your iPhone, iOS may download it from iCloud. That happens between your device and Apple, under Apple's privacy policy.

Photos are only deleted when you confirm it in iOS's own prompt. They move to Recently Deleted in Photos.

What stays on your device

Seconds keeps its analysis (photo groups and your keep or delete choices) and a few settings in the app's own storage on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes them.

To count free reviews, Seconds stores a small record in your device's Keychain: the date you first installed the app and which photo groups have used a free review. It contains no photos or personal details, is not synced to iCloud and is never sent to us. iOS may keep Keychain items after you delete an app.

What we don't do

Seconds has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. We don't sell or share data, because we don't collect any.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your name, email or payment details. The app checks on your device whether you have an active purchase.

If you contact us

If you write to us through the contact form, we use your name, email address and message only to reply to you. The message is delivered by our email provider, Resend, and kept in our mailbox for as long as needed to help you. We don't share it with anyone else.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of any personal data we hold about you, which in practice means support messages. Write to us through the contact form. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

Children

Seconds doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
