Seconds

Finds bursts and duplicates in your photo library, keeps the best shot and clears the rest.

How it works

Finds the doubles
Seconds groups bursts, near-identical shots and exact copies, including copies that were renamed, re-saved or sent through a messenger.
Picks the keeper
The best shot in each group is pre-selected on your iPhone. Your favorites always win. Change the pick with a tap.
Clears the rest
Deleted photos go to Recently Deleted in Photos, where you can restore them for 30 days. iOS asks you to confirm every delete.
Stays on your iPhone
No account, no uploads, no analytics, no ads. Your photos never leave your device.
