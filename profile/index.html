Profile

Sven Ahrens

Consumer growth leader and AI tinkerer based in Berlin. Previously Spotify, Yousician and Bain. Board observer and angel investor.

Summary

Sven Ahrens spent eight years at Spotify (2016 to 2024) through its growth years. He was responsible for more than $1B in subscription revenue, and as Director of Global Growth he led the team that drove more than 60% of Spotify's subscriber growth. From 2025 to 2026 he was CEO of Yousician, the company behind Yousician and GuitarTuna, with more than 20 million monthly active users.

He works on consumer subscription businesses end to end: acquisition, conversion, pricing and packaging, retention, international expansion and the organization behind them. He has done this at a scale of hundreds of millions of users and from zero. He builds his own AI products, Train with Kalle and TrainingZones.

Questions

What kind of roles does Sven Ahrens take on?
Operating roles (full-time, interim or fractional) leading growth, product, a region or a business unit; board seats, as member or observer; and advisory work with founders and executives.
Is he open to a full-time role?
He takes on full-time executive roles selectively, when the company, team and mandate fit.
What does he specialize in?
Consumer subscription growth: acquisition, conversion, pricing and packaging, retention and lifecycle, and international expansion, from early traction to hundreds of millions of users.
What is Newconic Ventures?
Sven Ahrens's angel investing vehicle, founded in 2019. It backs pre-seed and seed consumer, prosumer, AI and subscription companies in Europe and the US. Exits include Butter (acquired by Miro) and Petli (acquired by Agria).
Where is he based?
Berlin. He has worked in Berlin, Stockholm, New York, Helsinki and Munich, and speaks German, English, Spanish and Portuguese.
